About Kasma

Kasma Loha-unchit grew up in Thailand, outside Bangkok, learning to cook from her mother. She began teaching Thai cooking classes in the San Francisco Bay Area in 1985 and leading trips to Thailand in 1986, and wrote two cookbooks: It Rains Fishes, winner of the Julia Child Cookbook Award, and Dancing Shrimp. She retired from classes and trips in 2020 after 35 years.

Her story & cookbooks

About this archive

Kasma and her husband, Michael Babcock, put thaifoodandtravel.com online in 1998, and it grew into one of the web's deepest resources on Thai cooking. It hasn't seen new content for many years. This site brings the cooking material — recipes, ingredients, brands and articles — into a fast, searchable home, with the original pages' words and photographs.

Much more of the original site is still available at the Internet Archive, including the blog, trip journals and class menus. Pages there can load slowly, not every image was captured, and some blog slideshows no longer work — but it's well worth exploring.
